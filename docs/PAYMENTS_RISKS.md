# RISK REGISTER

P-01 The council service fee may be challenged as a tax in disguise. → Show it as "Proposed"; deduct it for real only with a by-law reference and a legal opinion reference; support a percentage or a fixed fee (rent bands later if the lawyer asks).
P-02 Landlords see double charging (property rate, income tax, council fee). → Clear wording that it pays for the registration, receipt and dispute service; legal opinion.
P-03 Avoidance through cash side-deals. → Fee based on the registered lease rent; only IBMT receipts count as proof in council disputes; modest deductions; human-led compliance only.
P-04 IBMT handling money would need a Bank of Tanzania licence. → IBMT has no account in the money path; record the partner's licence; ask the Bank of Tanzania.
P-05 GePG rules on bills and splitting. → Daily batch bill per council under its SP code; confirm with the Ministry of Finance; configurable.
P-06 Council system fee to IBMT: procurement, full fee to the council first, audit questions, late payment. → Procured contract; monthly invoice from reconciled GePG receipts; no deduction at source without written Treasury approval; configurable fee basis.
P-07 Partner rebate seen as a conflict of interest. → Disclosed to landlords and councils; never increases the landlord's deduction.
P-08 Payout fraud (account change or SIM swap). → OTP to the old number, cooling-off, notices, name check, joint-owner approval, two-person admin changes.
P-09 Mobile-money wallet limits. → Bank account option; held_limit state.
P-10 Confusion with TRA income tax. → Receipt wording; Stage 3 off.
P-11 Base44 cannot run real money. → Simulation only here; live only on a backend hosted in Tanzania after a penetration test.
P-12 Sensitive financial data. → Synthetic data only; masked account numbers; councils see fee records, never accounts; data processing agreement with the partner.
P-13 Forged or replayed callbacks, mismatched amounts. → Signature check, status query, duplicate rejection, alert queue, daily three-way reconciliation.
P-14 A new council leadership drops the scheme. → Contract term, notice period, exit plan.
P-15 Partner charges on deposits and refunds unknown. → Deposits and refunds stay outside the council fee until confirmed.
